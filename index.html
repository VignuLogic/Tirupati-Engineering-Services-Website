Tirupati Engineering Service is a trusted industrial surface treatment company based in Bharuch, Gujarat. Founded by Jaganath Subhanji, we specialize in sandblasting, epoxy coatings, chemical protection, passive fire protection, scaffolding, marine pile painting, and metallizing.

With our office in Bholav, Bharuch and a dedicated workshop in Sayakha Industrial Estate, we handle projects of all scales across the region.

What We Offer

Our Services

Complete industrial surface treatment — from rust removal to fire-resistant coatings, scaffolding, marine protection & metallizing.

⚙️
01
Sand / Shot Blasting
Removes rust, mill scale, and old coatings. Prepares metal surfaces to the ideal profile for long-lasting protective coatings.
🛡️
02
Epoxy Coating
Strong protection against corrosion for tanks, pipelines, and steel. Outstanding mechanical strength and chemical resistance.
🧪
03
Chemical Coating
Protects surfaces from acids, solvents, alkalis, and chemicals. Enhances durability in harsh industrial environments.
🔥
04
Fire Proofing (PFP)
Passive Fire Protection for structural steel. Slows heat absorption and maintains structural integrity during fire events.
🏗️
05
Scaffolding Work
Complete scaffolding erection and dismantling for industrial plants, refineries, and high-rise structures. Safe, fast, and compliant with all safety standards.
⚓
06
Marine Pile Painting
Specialized anti-corrosion and anti-fouling coatings for marine piles, jetties, and offshore steel structures exposed to seawater and tidal zones.
🔩
07
Metallizing
Thermal spray metallizing (zinc/aluminum) for superior long-term corrosion protection on structural steel, pipelines, and marine structures in extreme environments.
