What We Offer

Our 7 Services

Complete industrial surface treatment — from rust removal to fire-resistant coatings, scaffolding, marine protection & metallizing.

⚙️
01
Grit / Shot Blasting
Removes rust, mill scale, and old coatings. Prepares metal surfaces to the ideal profile for long-lasting protective coatings.
🛡️
02
Epoxy Coating
Strong protection against corrosion for tanks, pipelines, and steel. Outstanding mechanical strength and chemical resistance.
🧪
03
Chemical Coating
Protects surfaces from acids, solvents, alkalis, and chemicals. Enhances durability in harsh industrial environments.
🔥
04
Fire Proofing (PFP)
Passive Fire Protection for structural steel. Slows heat absorption and maintains integrity during fire events.
🏗️
05
Scaffolding Work
Complete scaffolding erection and dismantling for industrial plants, refineries, and high-rise structures. Safe, fast, fully compliant.
⚓
06
Marine Pile Painting
Specialized anti-corrosion coatings for marine piles, jetties, and offshore steel structures exposed to seawater.
🔩
07
Metallizing
Thermal spray metallizing (zinc/aluminum) for superior long-term corrosion protection on steel, pipelines, and marine structures.
